*The Polytechnic* is governed by three documents. The **Constitution** sets the structure, the **Bylaws** spell out duties and meeting rules, and the **Code of Ethics** sets the standard for how we report.

- [Constitution *Membership, the Editorial Board, elections, succession, removal, and finance.*](/governance/constitution)
- [Bylaws *Each editor's duties, and the rules for business, content, and endorsement meetings.*](/governance/bylaws)
- [Code of Ethics *Sourcing, plagiarism, recording, gifts, conflicts of interest, privacy, and corrections.*](/governance/code-of-ethics)
{.links-list}

## Quick reference

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Governing body** | The Editorial Board ([Constitution § IV](/governance/constitution#section-iv-committees)) |
| **Elections** | At the business meeting after the last day of classes, each semester ([§ V](/governance/constitution#section-v-meetings)) |
| **Election quorum** | Two-thirds of the voting constituency |
| **Joining the voting constituency** | Union member, contributed for six weeks, attended two consecutive business meetings, and told the EIC |
| **Endorsing a candidate** | Five-sixths vote of quorum ([Bylaws § IV](/governance/bylaws#section-iv-meetings)) |
| **Amending the Constitution** | Two-thirds of the voting constituency, plus Executive Board approval |
| **Passing a bylaw** | Two-thirds vote of a voting constituency quorum |
| **Ethics code** | Re-approved by the Editorial Board every semester |
| **Parliamentary authority** | *Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised* |
